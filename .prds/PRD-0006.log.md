## T-004 -- 2026-10-22

The flood tests (uat-005) started from the brief's bounds: an 8 MiB TCP round trip under the flood
within `max(4 × unloaded, unloaded + 5 s)`, and at least 90% of 200 exchanges (1 s each) answered.
Each test runs one session through `FakeRemote` and the real relay. The blaster sends 1 200-byte
datagrams in a tight loop and reads its echoes. The exchanging sender shares the blaster's UDP
forward and echo server, so its losses include drops in those sockets' kernel receive queues, which
nothing in sshunnel can share out fairly. All runs were debug builds, as `cargo make test` builds
them; `taskset` stood in for CI's 4-vCPU runners and a smaller one.

| Conditions | Runs | TCP unloaded | TCP under the flood | Worst ratio | Worst excess | Exchanges answered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 128 CPUs | 10 | 31.9 to 70.5 ms | 35.2 to 97.1 ms | 1.50× | +31 ms | 198 to 200 |
| `taskset` 4 CPUs | 10 | 31.4 to 41.5 ms | 66.9 to 80.8 ms | 2.41× | +46 ms | 199 to 200 |
| `taskset` 2 CPUs | 5 | 47.8 to 49.3 ms | 103.6 to 132.9 ms | 2.78× | +85 ms | 200 |
| `taskset` 4 CPUs, `cargo llvm-cov` (CI's coverage job) | 5 | 29.4 to 64.1 ms | 68.8 to 271.9 ms | 4.24× | +208 ms | 199 to 200 |

The flood was saturated throughout: on 4 CPUs its flow carried about 18 000 echoes a second (12 666
to 13 803 during 676 to 776 ms of exchanges) while the blaster offered several times that. During
each TCP transfer it echoed 400 to 670 datagrams, about one per stream frame: the writer taking
turns.

Tuned once:

- **TCP:** the floor tightened from +5 s to +2 s, about ten times the worst excess (+208 ms, under
  coverage). The 4× factor stays: that same run reached 4.24×, so the floor is what carries an
  instrumented run.
- **Exchanges:** at least 190 of 200 (95%), tightened from 90%. The worst run lost 2; the bound
  allows 10.

A listener that waited on its flow's full queue (a mutation, not kept) failed the starvation test.
